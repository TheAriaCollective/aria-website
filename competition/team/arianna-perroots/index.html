Founder and Masterclass Clinician

Arianna Perroots

Soprano

Arianna Perroots is a soprano and arts entrepreneur based in Las Vegas. Her career bridges performance, education, and business leadership. She founded a multi-city music school and built it from the ground up into a multi-million dollar business. In its second year, the school paid out one million dollars to local Las Vegas musicians.

Today she serves as Director of Logistics at a financial services firm. She brings that business and operational experience to every part of The Aria Collective.

As a performer, her roles include Suor Genovieffa in Puccini’s Suor Angelica and Morgana in Handel’s Alcina with Operativo, the soprano soloist in Golijov’s Ainadamar with the Moores Opera Center, Mlle. Silberklang in Mozart’s Der Schauspieldirektor, and both Cupid and Diana in Offenbach’s Orpheus in the Underworld. She has sung the national anthem for the Eighth Judicial District Court investiture ceremony. She holds a Master's degree in Vocal Performance and Pedagogy from the University of Houston and a Bachelor's degree in Vocal Performance from the University of Nevada, Las Vegas.

Arianna is a contributor to Classical Singer magazine and Eat More Art Vegas. She serves on the Board of Directors of Grace City Opera Theatre, which supports the promotion and performing of Black and African American operas around the US. She also serves on the board of VOX Vegas, the valley’s newest semi-professional choir. She is a passionate advocate for women in opera and the arts. Through The Aria Collective, she is working to strengthen opera's presence and accessibility in the Las Vegas community.
