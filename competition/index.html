Judges and team

Meet the team

Every singer performs before the judges and receives written feedback from esteemed industry professionals. More judges to be announced.

  • JudgeDr. Lisa ElliottSoprano · Conductor · EducatorDr. Lisa Elliott is an accomplished soprano and enjoys singing many styles, including opera, musical theater, and jazz standards. She has performed with various organizations, including Opera Las Vegas, Lyric Opera San Diego, and the Venetian Jazz Trio on the Las Vegas Strip.Read full bio
  • JudgeClaudia MerrillSoprano · Composer · EducatorFounder and Artistic Director of the Grace City Opera Theatre, Claudia has performed all over the United States and Europe singing opera and performing as a concert artist. After making her debut at Carnegie Hall singing John Rutter’s Requiem with the New England Symphony, she then went on to become the first African American Principal Soprano for the Las Vegas City Opera. Claudia has performed several roles and has performed at the Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts, Michigan Opera Theatre, Nevada Opera Theatre, Nevada Grand Opera and Arbor Opera Theatre. In addition to her opera work, Claudia has performed as a solo artist in Handel’s Messiah, Fauré’s Requiem, Dubois’ Seven Last Words of Christ, John Rutter’s Requiem, Mozart’s Magnificat, Vivaldi’s Gloria, Mendelssohn’s Elijah, John Carter’s Cantata, Joseph Joubert’s Oh Glory and many others.Read full bio
  • Founder and Masterclass ClinicianArianna PerrootsSopranoArianna Perroots is a soprano and arts entrepreneur based in Las Vegas. Her career bridges performance, education, and business leadership. She founded a multi-city music school and built it from the ground up into a multi-million dollar business. In its second year, the school paid out one million dollars to local Las Vegas musicians.Read full bio
Your day includes

From registration to the stage

  1. Register

    Complete your registration by Monday, February 15, 2027. Early registration is $100 before January 1, 2027, and $125 after. There is no audition recording and no screening round: register and you are guaranteed to sing.

  2. Rehearsal with your pianist

    Send your music ahead so your pianist can prepare. You'll receive your own arrival, rehearsal and performance times. Sing with one of our collaborative pianists, or bring your own: tell us ahead of time and we'll schedule you a 10-minute rehearsal. The fee is the same either way.

  3. Competition performance

    Perform before the judges at Grace Presbyterian Church on Saturday, April 17, 2027.

  4. Written feedback

    Every singer receives written feedback from esteemed industry professionals. Prizes are awarded in each division, and three singers will be selected for a masterclass with soprano Arianna Perroots.
