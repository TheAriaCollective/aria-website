The special award

Who is Grace City Opera?

Grace City Opera Theatre is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit opera company committed to the preservation, education and performance of the works of African American and African-descent classical composers and artists. It was founded in 2016 by soprano, composer and educator Claudia Merrill, one of this competition’s judges.

The company began in Farmington, Michigan. In October 2023 it opened in Nevada as the state’s first African American–founded opera company, and the first African American opera company in the state to be led by a woman.

In Las Vegas, Grace City Opera Theatre works with the City of North Las Vegas, the Las Vegas–Clark County Library District, the Martin Luther King Jr. Senior Center and the Nevada Ballet Theatre, through programs including Opera Roots, Create An Opera, Music As A Healer and Aspiring Artist.

At The Aria Collective Vocal Competition, Grace City Opera Theatre presents a special award.
