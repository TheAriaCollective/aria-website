About the Collective

Building a home for opera in Las Vegas

Las Vegas has world-class stages, world-class audiences and a deep pool of talented singers. Yet classical singers here have far fewer chances to compete, perform and grow than their peers on the East Coast.

The Aria Collective exists to change that. We believe Las Vegas can become a hub for classical singing. Our goal is to build a thriving community here, with real opportunities for the singers who live and train in this city. We want to bring a little of the East Coast to the West Coast.

  1. Now

    Competitions

    Live vocal competitions where every registered singer performs for professional judges. See the first annual Aria Collective University Vocal Competition.

  2. Next

    Scholarships

    Awards for training and private study, so promising singers can keep growing.

  3. The vision

    An opera house

    Our long-term goal is to build Las Vegas's first opera house.

Founder

Arianna Perroots

Arianna Perroots is a soprano and arts entrepreneur based in Las Vegas. Her career bridges performance, education, and business leadership. She founded a multi-city music school and built it from the ground up into a multi-million dollar business. In its second year, the school paid out one million dollars to local Las Vegas musicians.

Today she serves as Director of Logistics at a financial services firm. She brings that business and operational experience to every part of The Aria Collective.

As a performer, her recent roles include Morgana in Handel's Alcina, Suor Genovieffa in Puccini's Suor Angelica, and Cupid in Offenbach's Orpheus in the Underworld. She has sung the national anthem for the Eighth Judicial District Court investiture ceremony. She holds a Master's degree in Vocal Performance and Pedagogy from the University of Houston and a Bachelor's degree in Vocal Performance from the University of Nevada, Las Vegas.

Arianna is a contributor to Classical Singer magazine and Eat More Art Vegas. She serves on the Board of Directors of Grace City Opera Theatre, which supports the promotion and performing of Black and African American operas around the US. She is a passionate advocate for women in opera and the arts. Through The Aria Collective, she is working to strengthen opera's presence and accessibility in the Las Vegas community.
