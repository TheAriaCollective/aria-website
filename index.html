Our mission

Making Las Vegas a home for classical singers

Las Vegas has world-class stages and a deep pool of talented singers, but far fewer chances for classical singers to compete, perform and grow than their peers on the East Coast. We’re building those chances here.

More about the Collective
Founder

Arianna Perroots

Arianna Perroots is a soprano and arts entrepreneur based in Las Vegas. She founded a multi-city music school and built it from the ground up into a multi-million dollar business, and she brings that experience to every part of The Aria Collective.

She holds degrees in vocal performance from the University of Houston and the University of Nevada, Las Vegas, and serves on the boards of Grace City Opera Theatre and VOX Vegas.
